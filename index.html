Meet Crestview Healthcare Services

You are a new hire to Crestview's compliance team. A new law was signed into effect requiring Crestview to implement proper cyber and data protection practices in 90 days, or risk fines.

What the organization handles

  • Patient names, contact information, medical histories, diagnoses, prescriptions, insurance, and payment information
  • A cloud-based Electronic Health Record (EHR) system across four clinics
  • Third-party vendors supporting hosting, billing, and appointment scheduling

Current security challenges

  • A former employee still has access to the EHR system
  • Audit logs do not consistently track who accesses or modifies patient records
  • There is no formal security assessment process for third-party vendors
  • A critical vulnerability in an outdated patient system has remained unpatched for more than 30 days
  • Patient information is transmitted by email without consistent encryption

Hints for revision

One-page report guide

What your stakeholder report should say

  1. Executive recommendation: State whether Crestview is ready and name the three most important actions.
  2. Top risks: Explain what could happen, who or what is affected, and why it matters now.
  3. Selected controls: Connect each control to a regulation requirement and a risk.
  4. Evidence: Name the artifact that would prove each control is operating.
  5. Tradeoffs: Identify what you deferred, why you deferred it, and the residual risk.
  6. Success measures: Include at least two measurable targets, such as “100% of terminated users disabled within 24 hours.”
REAL WORLD PRESENTATION TIP:Lead with describing the risk and impacts to business- do not simply read a list of framework controls.
