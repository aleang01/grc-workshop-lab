One-page report guide

What your stakeholder report should say

  1. Executive recommendation: State whether Northstar is ready and name the three most important actions.
  2. Top risks: Explain what could happen, who or what is affected, and why it matters now.
  3. Selected controls: Connect each control to a regulation requirement and a risk.
  4. Evidence: Name the artifact that would prove each control is operating.
  5. Tradeoffs: Identify what you deferred, why you deferred it, and the residual risk.
  6. Success measures: Include at least two measurable targets, such as “100% of terminated users disabled within 24 hours.”
Presentation tipLead with risk and business impact. Do not simply read a list of framework controls.
